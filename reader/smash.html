SMASH: Physics-guided Reconstruction of Collisions from VideosSMASH：物理引导的视频碰撞重建

Abstract摘要

Collision sequences are commonly used in games and entertainment to add drama and excitement.

碰撞序列通常用于游戏和娱乐中，以增加戏剧性和刺激性。

Authoring even two body collisions in the real world can be difﬁcult, as one has to get timing and the object trajectories to be correctly synchronized.

即便只涉及两个物体，要在真实场景中安排好一次碰撞也很困难，因为必须准确协调时间和两者的运动轨迹。

After tedious trial-and-error iterations, when objects can actually be made to collide, then they are difﬁcult to capture in 3D.

经过繁琐的试错迭代后，当物体实际上可以发生碰撞时，它们就很难以 3D 形式捕捉。

In contrast, synthetically generating plausible collisions is difﬁcult as it requires adjusting different collision parameters (e.g., object mass ratio, coefﬁcient of restitution, etc.) and appropriate initial parameters.

相反，综合生成合理的碰撞很困难，因为它需要调整不同的碰撞参数（例如，物体质量比、恢复系数等）和适当的初始参数。

We present SMASH to directly read off appropriate collision parameters directly from raw input video recordings.

我们提出SMASH来直接从原始输入视频记录中直接读取适当的碰撞参数。

Technically we enable this by utilizing laws of rigid body collision to regularize the problem of lifting 2D trajectories to a physically valid 3D reconstruction of the collision.

技术上，我们利用刚体碰撞定律进行正则化，将二维轨迹提升为符合物理规律的三维碰撞重建。

The reconstructed sequences can then be modiﬁed and combined to easily author novel and plausible collisions.

然后可以修改和组合重建的序列，以轻松创作新颖且合理的碰撞。

We evaluate our system on a range of synthetic scenes and demonstrate the effectiveness of our method by accurately reconstructing several complex real world collision events.

我们在一系列合成场景上评估我们的系统，并通过准确重建几个复杂的现实世界碰撞事件来证明我们方法的有效性。

1 Introduction1 简介

Collisions capture suspense, build anticipation, and pack drama.

碰撞捕捉悬念，引发期待，并充满戏剧性。

Naturally, they remain an integral part of movies, games, and entertainment.

当然，它们仍然是电影、游戏和娱乐中不可或缺的一部分。

Creating a good real-world collision sequence involving multiple objects, however, is difﬁcult.

然而，创建涉及多个对象的良好现实世界碰撞序列是很困难的。

While the act of smashing two objects into each other so that they collide in a certain way is already non-trivial, the setup quickly becomes unmanageable when additional colliding objects are to be collided, or adjustments are required to the object trajectories.

仅让两个物体按预期方式相撞就已不易；若再加入其他物体或调整轨迹，实验设置会很快变得难以控制。

Such changes can easily require many further iterations and recordings, and become a tedious trial-and-error process.

此类更改很容易需要许多进一步的迭代和记录，并成为一个乏味的试错过程。

Moreover, trying out multiple collision iterations with expensive or fragile objects may not even be a realistic option.

此外，尝试与昂贵或脆弱的物体进行多次碰撞迭代甚至可能不是一个现实的选择。

Accurately capturing real-world collision sequences poses further challenges.

准确捕捉现实世界的碰撞序列提出了进一步的挑战。

On one hand, such sequences necessitate high to very high framerate capture, thus making state-of-the-art methods like Kinect Fusion, etc., unsuitable candidates for 3D acquisition.

一方面，此类序列需要高到非常高的帧速率捕获，因此使得 Kinect Fusion 等最先进的方法不适合 3D 采集。

On the other hand, even high-framerate video data only provides partial 2D information, both in space and in time (see Figure 2).

另一方面，即使是高帧率视频数据也只能提供空间和时间上的部分 2D 信息（见图 2）。

A fundamental problem arises due to unavoidable occlusions near collision instances, which prevents direct observation of the actual collision processes in any acquisition setup.

碰撞发生前后不可避免的遮挡构成了一项根本困难：无论采用何种采集配置，都难以直接观测碰撞过程本身。

While the physics of object collisions is a challenging problem in itself, well-developed high-level models exist to reduce its complexity.

虽然物体碰撞的物理学本身就是一个具有挑战性的问题，但成熟的高级模型可以降低其复杂性。

One widely used assumption is that of inﬁnite object stiffness, i.e., ideally rigid motions.

一种广泛使用的假设是无限物体刚度，即理想的刚性运动。

Whereas such rigid body simulations are widely used in games and movies, the task of setting up a collision with the right initial conditions remains tough: many parameters, such as velocities, mass ratios, and coefﬁcient of restitution, have to be correctly guessed and adjusted.

尽管此类刚体模拟在游戏和电影中广泛使用，但在正确的初始条件下设置碰撞的任务仍然很艰巨：必须正确猜测和调整许多参数，例如速度、质量比和恢复系数。

Given the nonlinear nature of the underlying physics, such a rigging up of a desirable sequence is problematic and typically requires extensive prior experience.

考虑到基础物理的非线性性质，这种理想序列的装配是有问题的，并且通常需要大量的先前经验。

In this work, we propose to marry the beneﬁts of the above setups.

在这项工作中，我们建议结合上述设置的优点。

The user records collisions between pairs of objects simply using a high framerate video (a smartphone in our setup).

用户只需使用高帧率视频（我们设置中的智能手机）即可记录成对物体之间的碰撞。

The video data, however, lacks depth information and is noisy.

然而，视频数据缺乏深度信息并且有噪声。

We ﬁrst automatically extract a set of candidate 3D positions from the video, and semi-automatically initialize a sparse set of orientations.

我们首先从视频中自动提取一组候选 3D 位置，并半自动初始化一组稀疏姿态。

We then formulate an optimization using regularization derived from conservation laws of rigid body collisions to reconstruct space-time trajectories of the participating objects.

然后，我们使用从刚体碰撞守恒定律导出的正则化来制定优化，以重建参与对象的时空轨迹。

This step utilizes the original 3D models of the colliding objects, assumed to be captured beforehand.

此步骤利用碰撞对象的原始 3D 模型，假设预先捕获。

As output, we can directly read off physical collision parameters from the reconstruction that can readily be incorporated into existing physics engines to recreate and reauthor modiﬁcations of the recorded collisions.

作为输出，我们可以直接从重建中读取物理碰撞参数，这些参数可以很容易地合并到现有的物理引擎中，以重新创建和重新创作对记录的碰撞的修改。

例如，在图 1 中，我们展示了幕后发生的重建碰撞序列的重播。

Note that we obtain high-quality reconstruction results even when the most relevant section (in time) of the original collision remains fully occluded in the input video.

请注意，即使原始碰撞的最相关部分（在时间上）在输入视频中仍然完全被遮挡，我们也可以获得高质量的重建结果。

We evaluate our method both on a range of synthetic evaluation setups and complex real-world collision examples, and generate new collision sequences using an easy authoring workﬂow.

我们在一系列综合评估设置和复杂的现实世界碰撞示例上评估我们的方法，并使用简单的创作工作流程生成新的碰撞序列。

In summary, we propose an algorithm to reconstruct physically valid collisions from an input video, and perform collision analysis without access to exact object geometry.

总之，我们提出了一种算法，可以从输入视频中重建物理上有效的碰撞，并在无需访问精确的对象几何形状的情况下执行碰撞分析。

The estimated collision parameters can then be used to author new collisions sequences.

然后可以使用估计的碰撞参数来创作新的碰撞序列。

2 Related work2 相关工作

Rigid body simulation has a long and successful history within Computer Graphics.

刚体模拟在计算机图形学领域有着悠久而成功的历史。

Starting with the early works in animation [Armstrong and Green 1985; Baraff 1990], they are now widely used in all forms of computer animation.

从早期的动画作品开始[阿姆斯特朗和格林 1985； Baraff 1990]，它们现在广泛应用于各种形式的计算机动画中。

A good overview can be found in the book by Eberly [2010].

可以在 Eberly [2010] 的书中找到很好的概述。

While the forward simulation problem is far from trivial, it is well studied.

虽然正向仿真问题并不简单，但已有较为充分的研究。

We are targeting an inverse problem, and in the following we restrict discussion to the relevant works.

我们针对的是逆问题，下面我们将讨论限制在相关工作中。

The ﬁrst methods to edit or modify simulations proposed spacetime methods to compute optimal motions in constrained systems [Witkin and Kass 1988; Liu et al. 1994].

第一种编辑或修改模拟的方法提出了计算约束系统中最佳运动的时空方法[Witkin and Kass 1988；刘等人。 1994]。

Variants with neural networks [Grzeszczuk et al. 1998] and genetic algorithms were proposed [Tang et al. 1995] to reduce the complexity or synthesize directable motions.

随后出现了结合神经网络 [Grzeszczuk et al. 1998] 和遗传算法 [Tang et al. 1995] 的变体，以降低复杂度或合成可控的运动。

A randomized approach to re-construct motions for target conﬁgurations of rigid bodies was proposed by Chenney et al. [2000], while an interactive variant was developed by Popovi´c et al. [2000].

Chenney 等人提出了一种随机方法来重建刚体目标配置的运动。 [2000]，而 Popovi´c 等人开发了一种交互式变体。 [2000]。

While these algorithms offer varying levels of control and parameter estimation for rigid bodies, they focus on virtual situations in a simulator.

虽然这些算法为刚体提供了不同级别的控制和参数估计，但它们侧重于模拟器中的虚拟情况。

In contrast, we propose a method that works robustly with only sparse, unreliable data from a real world source.

相比之下，我们提出了一种仅适用于来自现实世界源的稀疏、不可靠数据的稳健方法。

Areas that would be highly interesting, but which we have currently not taken into account are deformable objects [Terzopoulos et al. 1987; Martin et al. 2011] and fracture [M¨uller et al. 2001; Su et al. 2009].

可变形物体 [Terzopoulos et al. 1987; Martin et al. 2011] 和断裂 [Müller et al. 2001; Su et al. 2009] 也很值得研究，但尚未纳入本方法。

In the following, we assume that the observed objects do not deform or change topology.

在下文中，我们假设观察到的物体不会变形或改变拓扑。

Others have used the complexity of collision events to randomize the solution space, in this way giving control over the outcome of a simulation [Twigg and James 2007].

其他人利用碰撞事件的复杂性来随机化解空间，从而控制模拟的结果 [Twigg and James 2007]。

Recently, Smith et al. [2012] investigated the even tougher case of multiple, simultaneous collisions.

最近，史密斯等人 [2012] 研究了更棘手的多次同时碰撞的情况。

While we focus on the two body case, these papers highlight the complexities of collisions, which are ampliﬁed for imperfect real-world objects.

虽然我们关注的是两个物体的情况，但这些论文强调了碰撞的复杂性，对于不完美的现实世界物体，碰撞的复杂性被放大了。

A good general survey of collision modeling is the report by Gilardi and Sharf [2002].

Gilardi 和 Sharf [2002] 的报告是对碰撞建模的一个很好的综合概述。

An interesting general direction of research investigates the retrieval of modeling equations from data by employing learning techniques [Bongard and Lipson 2007].

一个有趣的一般研究方向是通过采用学习技术从数据中检索建模方程[Bongard and Lipson 2007]。

While our method is not directly using machine learning, it represents a data-driven approach to recover complex real-world phenomena.

虽然我们的方法不直接使用机器学习，但它代表了一种数据驱动的方法来恢复复杂的现实世界现象。

In this context, we demonstrate that suitable physical models are powerful regularizers for underdetermined problems.

在这一背景下，我们表明，合适的物理模型能够为欠定问题提供有效的正则化约束。

Conotter et al. [2012] analyze the kinematics of the ballistic motion of a single object in videos in the digital forensics context.

科诺特等人 [2012]在数字取证背景下分析视频中单个物体的弹道运动的运动学。

Salzmann and Urtasun [2011] explain the change in the linear motion of objects by solving for a sparse set of forces, applying their dynamics motivated regularization in video based tracking.

Salzmann 和 Urtasun [2011] 通过求解一组稀疏的力，并在基于视频的跟踪中应用其动力学驱动的正则化来解释物体线性运动的变化。

We focus on acquiring the intrinsic physical properties of the objects to guide our motion estimates taking both linear and angular motion into account.

我们专注于获取物体的内在物理属性，以指导我们的运动估计，同时考虑线性和角运动。

Geometry acquisition has been simpliﬁed with affordable and portable scanning options.

经济实惠的便携式扫描选项简化了几何采集。

Hence, in recent years, signiﬁcant efforts have gone towards capturing and parsing 3D scenes.

因此，近年来，人们在捕获和解析 3D 场景方面做出了巨大的努力。

Approaches include using classiﬁers on scene objects [Schlecht and Barnard 2009; Xiong and Huber 2010; Anand et al. 2013; Koppula et al. 2011; Silberman et al. 2012], interactive 3D modeling from raw RGBD scans [Shao et al. 2012], interleaving segmentation and classiﬁcation [Nan et al. 2012], unsupervised algorithms to identify and consolidate scans [Kim et al. 2012; Mattausch et al. 2014], dynamic reconstruction [Mitra et al. 2007], proxy geometry based scene understanding [Lafarge and Alliez 2013; Monszpart et al. 2015], or studying the spatial layout of scenes [Gupta et al. 2010; Lee et al. 2010; Hartley et al. 2012].

方法包括在场景对象上使用分类器 [Schlecht 和 Barnard 2009；熊和胡贝尔，2010；阿南德等人。 2013年；科普拉等人。 2011；西尔伯曼等人。 2012]，根据原始 RGBD 扫描进行交互式 3D 建模 [Shao 等人。 2012]，交错分割和分类[Nan 等人。 2012]，识别和整合扫描的无监督算法[Kim 等人。 2012年；马陶什等人。 2014]，动态重建[Mitra 等人。 2007]，基于代理几何的场景理解[Lafarge and Alliez 2013；蒙斯帕特等人。 2015]，或研究场景的空间布局[Gupta 等人。 2010；李等人。 2010；哈特利等人。 2012]。

Physical constraints have also been used for scene understanding: for example, [Jia et al. 2013; Jiang and Xiao 2013; Zheng et al. 2013; Shao* and Monszpart* et al. 2014] consider local and global physical stability to predict occluded geometry in scenes.

物理约束也已用于场景理解：例如，[Jia 等人。 2013年；江、肖，2013；郑等人。 2013年； Shao* 和 Monszpart* 等人。 2014]考虑局部和全局物理稳定性来预测场景中被遮挡的几何体。

These methods, however, primarily focus on static scenes.

然而，这些方法主要关注静态场景。

In case of dynamic scenes, even state-of-the-art RGBD based systems [Newcombe et al. 2015; Dou et al. 2015] struggle with highly dynamic motion and occlusion.

在动态场景的情况下，即使是最先进的基于 RGBD 的系统 [Newcombe 等人。 2015年；窦等人。 2015] 与高动态运动和遮挡作斗争。

Capturing 3D geometry of dynamic scenes remains very challenging.

捕捉动态场景的 3D 几何仍然非常具有挑战性。

For dynamic scenarios, inverse methods with physics priors have also been used to capture other phenomena such as liquids [Wang et al. 2009] or smokes [Gregson et al. 2014].

在动态场景中，带物理先验的反演方法也用于恢复液体 [Wang et al. 2009] 和烟雾 [Gregson et al. 2014] 等现象。

While impressive results have been demonstrated in case of template-based solutions for human faces, hair, body, etc., they focus on application speciﬁc contexts where object behavior and dynamics can be captured and learned in a training phase.

虽然在针对人脸、头发、物体等的基于模板的解决方案中已经展示了令人印象深刻的结果，但它们专注于特定于应用程序的上下文，在这些上下文中可以在训练阶段捕获和学习对象行为和动态。

In contrast, we focus on reconstructing 3D geometry of collisions directly from raw videos.

相比之下，我们专注于直接从原始视频重建碰撞的 3D 几何形状。

Note that due to the nature of the problem, collisions happen prohibitively fast for accurate capture.

请注意，由于问题的性质，碰撞发生的速度非常快，无法准确捕获。

Further, object parts are severely occluded near the actual collision.

此外，物体部分在实际碰撞附近被严重遮挡。

We demonstrate, that motion states of the objects as well as internal physical properties can be estimated by analysing the objects’ pre- and post-collision trajectories away from the time of collision.

我们证明，只需分析远离碰撞瞬间的碰撞前后轨迹，便可估计物体运动状态及其内在物理属性。

3 Formulation3 问题建模

Our goal is to acquire pairwise relevant parameters of object collisions simply based on an input video and reconstruct a physically valid motion.

5.5 Limitations and Discussion5.5 限制和讨论

A key limitation of our method is that it only applies to rigid body collisions.

我们的方法的一个关键限制是它仅适用于刚体碰撞。

In case of deformable bodies, the physical models we employ can be unsuitable.

对于可变形体，我们采用的物理模型可能不合适。

Empirically, however, we observed that our estimates are still reasonable for near rigid collisions (see Figure 17).

然而，根据经验，我们观察到我们的估计对于近刚性碰撞仍然是合理的（见图 17）。

Our algorithm in its current form can reconstruct real-world effects under the assumption of zero friction, but including a friction model in our optimization could potentially increase the accuracy of our reconstructions.

我们当前形式的算法可以在零摩擦的假设下重建现实世界的效果，但在我们的优化中包含摩擦模型可能会提高重建的准确性。

While our current pipeline still requires user input (2 orientations per parabola segment), it is worth pointing out that the user-annotated orientations represent only a small part of the overall input data.

虽然我们当前的流程仍然需要用户输入（每个抛物线段 2 个姿态），但值得指出的是，用户标注的姿态仅代表整个输入数据的一小部分。

All the 3D positions of the objects are reconstructed fully automatically.

物体的所有 3D 位置都是全自动重建的。

The user input is typically only required to reﬁne the orientations, which are important for angular motions, and thus help to retrieve the physical parameters.

用户输入通常只需要细化姿态，这对于角运动很重要，从而有助于检索物理参数。

Also, as we only rely on a single input video, we need enough visual information about the collision event to be visible.

此外，由于我们仅依赖于单个输入视频，因此我们需要足够的有关碰撞事件的视觉信息才能可见。

For example, if a collision is happening purely along a view direction ray, our method will most likely underestimate the objects’ velocities.

例如，如果碰撞纯粹沿着视线方向射线发生，我们的方法很可能会低估物体的速度。

Likewise, objects that move very quickly might not exhibit enough change of direction from gravity.

同样，移动速度非常快的物体可能不会因重力而表现出足够的方向变化。

In such a case, our method requires more accurate centroids to reliably recover a depth estimate from gravity.

在这种情况下，我们的方法需要更准确的质心才能可靠地从重力恢复深度估计。

Lastly, it is noticeable in some reconstructions (such as Figure 14a) that the objects intersect each other at collision time.

最后，在一些重建中（如图 14a）值得注意的是，对象在碰撞时彼此相交。
