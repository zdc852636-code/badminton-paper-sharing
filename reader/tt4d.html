Abstract.摘要

We present TT4D , a large-scale, high-fidelity table tennis dataset.

本文提出 TT4D，一个大规模、高保真的乒乓球数据集。

It provides 140+ hours of reconstructed singles and doubles gameplay from monocular broadcast videos, featuring annotations like high-quality camera calibrations, precise 3D ball positions, ball spin, time segmentation, and 3D human meshes over time.

它从单目转播视频中重建了超过 140 小时的单打和双打比赛，并提供高质量相机标定、精确三维球位置、球旋转、时间分段以及随时间变化的三维人体网格等标注。

This rich data provides a new foundation for virtual replay, in-depth player analysis, and robot learning.

这些丰富的数据为虚拟回放、深入的球员分析和机器人学习提供了新的基础。

The dataset’s combination of scale and precision is achieved through a novel reconstruction pipeline.

数据集兼具规模与精度，得益于一套新的重建流程。

Prior methods first partition a game sequence into individual shot segments using the 2D ball track, and only then attempt reconstruction.

已有方法先根据二维球轨迹将比赛序列分割为各个击球片段，再尝试进行三维重建。

However, 2D-based time segmentation collapses under occlusion and varied camera viewpoints, preventing reliable reconstruction.

然而，基于二维轨迹的时间分段在遮挡和不同机位下容易失效，从而妨碍可靠重建。

We invert this paradigm by first lifting the entire unsegmented 2D ball track to 3D through a learned lifting network.

我们将这一顺序反转：先通过学习到的提升网络，将整段未经分割的二维球轨迹提升到三维。

This 3D trajectory then allows us to reliably perform time segmentation.

随后，利用得到的三维轨迹可靠地完成时间分段。

The learned lifting network also infers the ball’s spin, handles unreliable ball detections, and successfully reconstructs the ball trajectory in cases of high occlusion.

该提升网络还推断球的旋转、处理不可靠的检测结果，并在严重遮挡时成功重建球轨迹。

This lift-first design is necessary, as our pipeline is the only method capable of reconstructing table tennis gameplay from general-view broadcast monocular videos.

这种“先提升”的设计至关重要：作者认为，本文流程是唯一能从一般视角的单目转播视频中重建乒乓球比赛的方法。

We demonstrate the dataset’s fidelity through two downstream tasks: estimating the racket’s pose & velocity at impact, and training a generative model of competitive rallies.

我们通过两个下游任务展示数据集的保真度：估计击球瞬间的球拍姿态与速度，以及训练竞技回合的生成模型。

1. Introduction1. 引言

Online platforms host a vast and growing collection of high-quality competitive sports footage.

在线平台拥有大量且不断增长的高质量竞技体育镜头。

This abundance of broadcast video makes 4D monocular-view reconstruction a scalable task enabling virtual replay, player analytics, and robot learning.

如此丰富的转播视频使得 4D 单目视图重建成为一项可扩展的任务，支持虚拟重播、球员分析和机器人学习。

Table tennis, in particular, serves as a challenging testbed due to its high-speed and dynamic nature.

尤其是乒乓球运动，由于其高速和动态的特性，成为一个具有挑战性的测试平台。

A complete analysis goes beyond human mesh recovery and ball position reconstruction: it includes estimating ball spin, which strongly influences both flight (via the Magnus effect) and bounce behavior.

完整的分析超出了人体网格恢复和球位置重建的范围：它包括估计球的旋转，这强烈影响飞行（通过马格努斯效应）和弹跳行为。

Extracting these signals at scale from broadcast video is difficult: the ball is small, moves rapidly, and is routinely occluded by players.

从转播视频中大规模提取这些信号很困难：球很小，移动迅速，并且经常被球员遮挡。

This constant occlusion makes time segmentation, the task of identifying the exact moment of a hit, the biggest challenge for reconstruction.

这种持续的遮挡使得时间分割（识别准确的击中时刻的任务）成为重建的最大挑战。

Existing pipelines ( Etaat et al., 2025 ; Gossard et al., 2025 ; Ertner et al., 2024 ; Kienzle et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) follow a 2D-based strategy: first partition the sequence into shot segments using the 2D ball track, then lift each segment to 3D.

现有的流程（Etaat 等人，2025；Gossard 等人，2025；Ertner 等人，2024；Kienzle 等人，2025；Kienzle 等人，2026）遵循基于 2D 的策略：首先使用 2D 球轨迹将序列划分为击球段，然后将每个段提升到 3D。

Automated 2D-based segmentation, as in LATTE-MV ( Etaat et al., 2025 ) and TT3D ( Gossard et al., 2025 ) , often fails when the 2D track is interrupted by occlusions or corrupted by misdetections.

基于自动 2D 的分割，如 LATTE-MV（Etaat 等人，2025）和 TT3D（Gossard 等人，2025），当 2D 轨道因遮挡而中断或因误检测而损坏时，通常会失败。

Manual segmentation ( Kienzle et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) allows for precise benchmarking but is not scalable, feasible only for small test sets.

手动分割（Kienzle et al., 2025；Kienzle et al., 2026）允许精确的基准测试，但不可扩展，仅适用于小型测试集。

In this work, we introduce the Lift-First Pipeline , which fundamentally reverses this logic.

在这项工作中，我们引入了 Lift-First Pipeline ，它从根本上扭转了这一逻辑。

Our pipeline directly lifts the entire unsegmented 2D ball track to a continuous 3D trajectory using a learned model.

我们的流程使用学习模型直接将整个未分段的 2D 球轨迹提升为连续的 3D 轨迹。

Only then do we perform time segmentation in the unambiguous 3D domain.

只有这样我们才能在明确的 3D 域中执行时间分段。

Once this 3D trajectory is available, hit and bounce events can be reliably identified.

一旦获得该 3D 轨迹，就可以可靠地识别撞击和弹跳事件。

Our Lift-First Pipeline is enabled by our core technical contribution: a novel Full-Sequence Lifting Network .

我们的 Lift-First Pipeline 是通过我们的核心技术贡献实现的：一种新颖的全序列三维链路网络。

By taking a 3D-first approach, we can process long and complex unsegmented gameplay for the first time.

通过采用 3D 优先的方法，我们可以首次处理冗长且复杂的未分段游戏玩法。

This network is trained on a massive-scale synthetic dataset of 3 million rallies.

该网络在 300 万个回合的大规模合成数据集上进行训练。

We summarize our contributions as follows:

我们的贡献总结如下：

  • •

    The “Lift-First" Reconstruction Pipeline: A new paradigm that decouples 3D reconstruction from fragile 2D-based time segmentation by first lifting the entire unsegmented sequence to 3D.

    “Lift-First”重建流程：一种新范例，通过首先将整个未分段序列三维提升到 3D，将 3D 重建与脆弱的基于 2D 的时间分段解耦。

  • •

    A Novel Full-Sequence Lifting Network: The core technical method, enabled by a 3M points synthetic dataset.

    新颖的全序列三维提升网络：核心技术方法，由 3M 点合成数据集启用。

    The network is the first to process unsegmented rallies and outputs the full 3D trajectory and dense per-frame 3D spin vectors.

    该网络是第一个处理未分段的循环并输出完整的 3D 轨迹和密集的每帧 3D 自旋向量的网络。

  • •

    The TT4D Dataset: A 140+ hour dataset generated with our pipeline, featuring precise 3D ball trajectories, 3D human meshes, and two annotations previously unavailable at scale: dense ball spin and robust 3D-derived time segmentations.

    TT4D 数据集：使用我们的流程生成的 140 多个小时的数据集，具有精确的 3D 球轨迹、3D 人体网格以及以前无法大规模使用的两个标注：密集的球旋转和强大的 3D 衍生时间分段。

  • •

    Novel Downstream Applications: We demonstrate our dataset’s high fidelity by (a) introducing a new racket stroke estimation method that recovers the racket’s pose and velocity at impact and (b) training a generative Flow Matching model on competitive gameplay.

    新颖的下游应用：我们通过以下方式展示了数据集的高保真度：(a) 引入一种新的球拍行程估计方法，该方法可以恢复球拍撞击时的姿态和速度；(b) 训练竞技比赛中的生成流匹配模型。

We will release the TT4D dataset, paving the way for new applications in computational sports science and robotics ( Etaat et al., 2025 ; Wang et al., 2012 ; Wang et al., 2017 ) .

我们将发布 TT4D 数据集，为计算运动科学和机器人学的新应用铺平道路（Etaat 等，2025；Wang 等，2012；Wang 等，2017）。

2. Related Work2.相关工作

2D ball tracking in table tennis is challenging due to its small size, fast motion, frequent occlusions, and motion blur.

乒乓球运动中的 2D 球跟踪由于其尺寸小、运动快、频繁遮挡和运动模糊而具有挑战性。

Recent methods rely on deep detectors ( Huang et al., 2019 ; Zandycke and Vleeschouwer, 2019 ; Komorowski et al., 2019 ; Sun et al., 2020 ) , with the Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (MIMO) formulation from TrackNetV2 ( Sun et al., 2020 ) being a key breakthrough.

最近的方法依赖于深度检测器（Huang et al., 2019；Zandycke and Vleeschouwer, 2019；Komorowski et al., 2019；Sun et al., 2020），TrackNetV2 的多输入多输出（MIMO）公式（Sun et al., 2020）是一个关键突破。

This MIMO strategy has been adopted by subsequent works using different backbones ( Tarashima et al., 2023 ; Liu and Wang, 2022 ; Chen and Wang, 2024 ; Kienzle et al., 2024 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) .

这种 MIMO 策略已被使用不同骨干网的后续工作采用（Tarashima 等人，2023；Liu 和 Wang，2022；Chen 和 Wang，2024；Kienzle 等人，2024；Kienzle 等人，2026）。

Attention mechanisms have also been incorporated to enhance temporal feature fusion ( Hu et al., 2018 ; Gossard et al., 2026 ; Raj et al., 2025 ) .

还引入了注意力机制来增强时间特征融合（Hu et al., 2018；Gossard et al., 2026；Raj et al., 2025）。

Methods for 3D trajectory lifting can be split into physics-based optimization and learned networks.

3D 轨迹三维链接的方法可以分为基于物理的优化和学习网络。

Optimization-based methods like TT3D ( Gossard et al., 2025 ) , LATTE-MV ( Etaat et al., 2025 ) and MonoTrack ( Liu and Wang, 2022 ) minimize reprojection error.

基于优化的方法，如 TT3D（Gossard 等人，2025）、LATTE-MV（Etaat 等人，2025）和 MonoTrack（Liu 和 Wang，2022）可最大限度地减少重投影误差。

These non-learning methods are inherently limited to the “Traditional Pipeline" and hence do not scale.

这些非学习方法本质上仅限于“传统流程”，因此无法扩展。

Their optimization is already unstable for single, shot segments; extending them to a “3D-first" approach that jointly solves for the trajectory, all unknown bounce points, and all unknown hit-points is computationally infeasible.

它们的优化对于单个击球段来说已经不稳定；将它们扩展到“3D优先”的方法来联合求解轨迹、所有未知的弹跳点和所有未知的击球点在计算上是不可行的。

This necessitates a learning-based approach to make the Lift-First Pipeline viable.

这就需要一种基于学习的方法来使提升优先流程变得可行。

Recent learned approaches train a network to lift 2D tracks to 3D trajectories.

最近学习的方法训练网络将 2D 轨道提升到 3D 轨迹。

SynthNet ( Ertner et al., 2024 ) and ( Ponglertnapakorn and Suwajanakorn, 2025 ) both tackle this for tennis.

SynthNet（Ertner 等人，2024）和（Ponglertnapakorn 和 Suwajanakorn，2025）都在网球领域解决了这个问题。

Most relevant is the work of Kienzle et al. (2025) , which proposes a transformer that lifts single shot segments from 2D to 3D with zero-shot generalization from synthetic to real data.

最相关的是 Kienzle 等人的工作。 (2025)，它提出了一种Transformer，可以将单个击球段从 2D 提升到 3D，并从合成数据到真实数据进行零击球泛化。

This was extended in ( Kienzle et al., 2026 ) and serves as the basis for our network.

这在（Kienzle et al., 2026）中得到了扩展，并作为我们网络的基础。

However, all these methods still depend on the “Traditional Pipeline" and its dependence on fragile 2D-based time segmentation.

然而，所有这些方法仍然依赖于“传统流程”及其对脆弱的基于 2D 的时间分割的依赖。

We bypass this limitation and develop a network that can lift full rallies from 2D to 3D.

我们绕过了这个限制，开发了一个可以将完整循环从 2D 提升到 3D 的网络。

Spin estimation methods include indirect inference from trajectories or direct logo tracking ( Tebbe et al., 2020 ) .

自旋估计方法包括从轨迹间接推断或直接标识跟踪（Tebbe 等人，2020）。

Direct tracking has been improved with custom dot patterns ( Gossard et al., 2023 ) and event cameras that mitigate motion blur ( Gossard et al., 2024 ; Nakabayashi et al., 2024 ) .

通过自定义点图案（Gossard 等人，2023）和减轻运动模糊的事件相机（Gossard 等人，2024；Nakabayashi 等人，2024），直接跟踪得到了改进。

These hardware-specific methods are complemented by works that classify spin from player stroke motion ( Kulkarni and Shenoy, 2021 ; Fujihara et al., 2025 ) .

这些特定于硬件的方法得到了对球员击球运动中的旋转进行分类的工作的补充（Kulkarni 和 Shenoy，2021；Fujihara 等人，2025）。

Most recently, Kienzle et al. (2025) ; Kienzle et al. (2026) showed that 2D-3D lifting transformers can also regress the initial 3D spin vector.

最近，Kienzle 等人。 (2025);金茨勒等人。 (2026) 表明 2D-3D 三维通道Transformer还可以回归初始 3D 自旋向量。

We adapt this to predict per-frame spin for an entire unsegmented rally.

我们调整它来预测整个未分段循环的每帧旋转。

Specialized table-tennis datasets have only emerged recently.

专门的乒乓球数据集最近才出现。

Blurball ( Gossard et al., 2026 ) provided blur-aware 2D annotations, while TT3D ( Gossard et al., 2025 ) offered precise 3D trajectories from a multi-camera setup.

Blurball（Gossard 等人，2026）提供了模糊感知的 2D 标注，而 TT3D（Gossard 等人，2025）则通过多摄像机设置提供精确的 3D 轨迹。

Synthetic datasets of individual shot segments have been used for model training ( Kienzle et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) , along with small-scale real-world sets with topspin/backspin annotations ( Kienzle et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) .

各个击球片段的合成数据集已用于模型训练（Kienzle 等人，2025；Kienzle 等人，2026），以及带有上旋/后旋标注的小规模现实世界数据集（Kienzle 等人，2025；Kienzle 等人，2026）。

TTNet ( Voeikov et al., 2020 ) and P2ANet ( Bian et al., 2024 ) primarily focus on event spotting and fine-grained action detection within broadcast or multi-task video contexts.

TTNet（Voeikov 等人，2020）和 P2ANet（Bian 等人，2024）主要关注广播或多任务视频环境中的事件发现和细粒度动作检测。

Most similar to our TT4D dataset in scale is LATTE-MV ( Etaat et al., 2025 ) , which reconstructed 26 hours of gameplay using the “Traditional Pipeline”.

在规模上与我们的 TT4D 数据集最相似的是 LATTE-MV（Etaat 等人，2025），它使用“传统流程”重建了 26 小时的游戏时间。

Our TT4D dataset, enabled by our Lift-First Pipeline, surpasses this by an order of magnitude.

我们的 TT4D 数据集由 Lift-First Pipeline 支持，超出了这个数量级。

Beyond scale, TT4D provides higher-fidelity data, using an improved camera calibration method and providing realistic 3D trajectories, in contrast to LATTE-MV’s simplified parabolic fits.

除了比例之外，TT4D 还提供了更高保真度的数据，使用改进的相机标定方法并提供逼真的 3D 轨迹，与 LATTE-MV 的简化抛物线拟合形成鲜明对比。

Crucially, it is the first to provide two key annotations at this scale: dense, per-frame 3D spin and robust 3D-derived time segmentation, which are reliable even when 2D occlusions break prior methods.

至关重要的是，它是第一个在这种规模上提供两个关键标注的技术：密集的每帧 3D 旋转和强大的 3D 衍生时间分段，即使 2D 遮挡破坏了先前的方法，这些标注也是可靠的。

3. Methodology: The Lift-First Pipeline3. 方法论：提升优先流程

3.1. Terminology3.1.术语

We first define terminology used throughout the paper:

我们首先定义整篇论文中使用的术语：

  • •

    A Segment starts when one player hits the ball and ends when another player hits the ball.

    当一名球员击球时，一个分段开始，当另一名球员击球时结束。

  • •

    A Point starts with a serve and ends when the ball is first out of play.

    得分从发球开始，到球首先出界时结束。

    Segments partition the point into disjoint time intervals.

    段将点划分为不相交的时间间隔。

  • •

    A Game is a sequence of points.

    游戏是一系列的点。

    It is complete when one team achieves the winning score.

    当一支球队获得获胜分数时，比赛就完成了。

  • •

    (2D/3D)-based Time Segmentation partitions a point into segments using 2D or 3D information.

    基于 (2D/3D) 的时间分段使用 2D 或 3D 信息将点划分为片段。

  • •

    4D Reconstruction recovers camera parameters, 3D ball positions, ball spin, and 3D human meshes over time .

    4D 重建可随时间恢复摄像机参数、3D 球位置、球旋转和 3D 人体网格。

3.2. Pipeline Overview3.2.流程概览

Conventional table tennis reconstruction pipelines first perform 2D-based time segmentation of a point and then reconstruct each segment independently ( Etaat et al., 2025 ; Gossard et al., 2025 ) .

传统的乒乓球重建流程首先对点进行基于 2D 的时间分割，然后独立重建每个片段（Etaat 等人，2025；Gossard 等人，2025）。

However, this time segmentation scheme is highly sensitive to occlusions and missing detections, limiting scalability.

然而，这种时间分割方案对遮挡和缺失检测高度敏感，限制了可扩展性。

We instead adopt a Lift-First Pipeline (Fig. 2 ).

相反，我们采用了 Lift-First Pipeline（提升优先流程）（图 2）。

Rather than segmenting first, we lift the entire unsegmented point to 3D and perform time segmentation and annotation directly in the 3D domain, where the ball trajectory signal is unambiguous.

我们不是首先进行分割，而是将整个未分割的点提升到 3D 并直接在 3D 域中执行时间分割和标注，其中球轨迹信号是明确的。

The pipeline consists of four stages:

该流程由四个阶段组成：

  1. (1)

    Data Acquisition and Preprocessing ( Section 3.3 ): clipping the points of a game from broadcast footage, calibrating cameras, extracting 2D ball tracks, and estimating 3D human meshes ( Loper et al., 2015 ) .

    数据采集和预处理（第 3.3 节）：从广播镜头中剪辑比赛要点、校准摄像机、提取 2D 球轨迹以及估计 3D 人体网格（Loper 等人，2015）。

  2. (2)

    Full 3D Lifting ( Section 3.4 ): a transformer-based network predicts dense 3D ball trajectories and per-frame spin for the full point from the 2D track.

    全 3D 三维提升（第 3.4 节）：基于Transformer的网络预测 2D 轨迹完整回合的密集 3D 球轨迹和每帧旋转。

  3. (3)

    3D-Domain Annotation ( Section 3.5 ): segments, hit points, bounces, and racket-contact parameters are computed from the reconstructed 3D trajectory.

    3D 域标注（第 3.5 节）：根据重建的 3D 轨迹计算段、击球点、弹跳和球拍接触参数。

  4. (4)

    Filtering and Curation ( Section 3.6 ): 2D and 3D consistency checks ensure that all retained trajectories are visually and physically plausible.

    过滤和管理（第 3.6 节）：2D 和 3D 一致性检查确保所有保留的轨迹在视觉和物理上都是合理的。

3.3. Stage 1: Data Acquisition and Preprocessing3.3.第一阶段：数据采集和预处理

Our pipeline begins with raw, multi-hour uncut table tennis videos of full games.

我们的流程从完整比赛的原始、多小时未剪辑的乒乓球视频开始。

We apply a two-stage clipping process.

我们应用两阶段裁剪过程。

The first stage splits the games into individual points by detecting scoreboard changes using YOLO ( Varghese and M., 2024 ) and PaddleOCR ( Cui et al., 2025 ) .

第一阶段通过使用 YOLO（Varghese 和 M.，2024）和 PaddleOCR（Cui 等人，2025）检测记分牌变化，将比赛分为各个分数。

The second stage identifies the approximate start and end of the point using 2D ball track oscillations using a ball tracker.

第二阶段使用球跟踪器使用 2D 球轨迹振荡来识别该点的大致起点和终点。

We then process all clips to detect and remove duplicated frames, a common artifact in broadcast video that corrupts trajectory estimation.

然后，我们处理所有剪辑以检测并删除重复的帧，这是转播视频中常见的伪影，会破坏轨迹估计。

Our method uses SSIM ( SSIM ) ( Wang et al., 2004 ) to identify these frames.

我们的方法使用 SSIM (SSIM) (Wang et al., 2004) 来识别这些帧。

For each resulting valid clip, we extract the following information:

对于每个生成的有效剪辑，我们提取以下信息：

  • •

    Camera calibration: We follow TT3D ( Gossard et al., 2025 ) , solving a Perspective- n -Point problem from table corners with unknown focal length, and improving robustness through enhanced table segmentation and temporal filtering.

    相机标定：沿用 TT3D（Gossard 等，2025）的方法，根据球台角点求解焦距未知的透视 n 点问题，并通过改进球台分割及时间滤波提高鲁棒性。

  • •

    2D ball detections: TrackNetV3 ( Chen and Wang, 2024 ) is applied without its inpainting module.

    2D 球检测：TrackNetV3（Chen 和 Wang，2024）在没有修复模块的情况下应用。

  • •

    3D human meshes: We use 4DHumans ( Goel et al., 2023 ) and align the meshes to the world frame.

    3D 人体网格：我们使用 4DHumans（Goel 等人，2023）并将网格与世界坐标系对齐。

Additional implementation and parameter details are provided in the supplementary material.

补充材料中提供了其他实现和参数详细信息。

3.4. Stage 2: Full-Sequence Lifting Network3.4.第二阶段：全序列三维提升网络

The central component of our pipeline is a transformer-based Full-Sequence Lifting Network.

本流程的核心是基于 Transformer 的全序列三维提升网络。

For each point consisting of N frames, it processes the sequence of 2D ball detections {r→2​D​(tn)}n=0N−1 , their corresponding timestamps {tn}n=0N−1 , and a set of 2D table keypoints {k→i}i=113 that are derived from the camera calibration.

对于包含 N 帧的每个回合，网络处理二维球检测序列 {r→2​D​(tn)}n=0N−1、对应时间戳 {tn}n=0N−1，以及由相机标定获得的二维球台关键点 {k→i}i=113。

The network infers the 3D trajectory {r→ 3​D​(tn)}n=0N−1 and 3D spin vectors {ω→​(tn)}n=0N−1 for each frame.

网络为每一帧推断三维轨迹 {r→ 3​D​(tn)}n=0N−1 和三维旋转向量 {ω→​(tn)}n=0N−1。

Our network is built upon the baseline lifting model from Kienzle et al. (2026) , which demonstrated strong generalization to real videos despite being trained solely on synthetic data.

我们的网络以 Kienzle 等人（2026）的三维提升模型为基础；该模型虽然仅用合成数据训练，却能很好地泛化到真实视频。

We retain its key innovations, such as using Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE) ( Su et al., 2024 ) based on exact timestamps to handle varying frame rates and missing 2D ball detections ( Kienzle et al., 2026 ) .

我们保留了其关键设计，例如基于精确时间戳的旋转位置编码 RoPE（Su 等，2024），以应对不同帧率和二维球检测缺失（Kienzle 等，2026）。

However, the baseline model is designed for a “Traditional Pipeline”: it processes isolated, pre-segmented shots, predicts only a single initial spin vector per segment, and handles missing 2D ball detections by simply discarding them.

但该基线面向“传统流程”：只处理独立且预先分割好的击球片段，每段仅预测一个初始旋转向量，并直接丢弃缺少二维球检测的帧。

This is insufficient for our “Lift-First Pipeline,” which must process unsegmented points of arbitrary length, generate dense spin estimates, and actively reconstruct occluded detections to enable precise 3D-based temporal segmentation.

这些能力不足以支撑“先提升流程”：后者必须处理任意长度、未经分割的回合，生成逐帧旋转估计，并主动恢复遮挡处的状态，以支持精确的三维时间分段。

Therefore, we introduce three key contributions to solve this: a massive-scale synthetic training dataset of full points, architectural extensions for modeling dense spin, and an interpolation token that enables predictions for missed detections.

为此，我们引入三项关键改进：包含完整回合的大规模合成训练数据集、用于预测逐帧旋转的网络结构扩展，以及支持漏检位置预测的插值词元。

Synthetic Dataset.

合成数据集。

To learn the dynamics of continuous play, we require training data that reflects the complexity of full, unsegmented points, not just isolated segments as is done in ( Kienzle et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) .

为学习连续比赛的运动规律，训练数据必须体现完整、未经分割回合的复杂性，而不能只包含 Kienzle 等人（2025、2026）使用的独立片段。

We therefore generate a new massive-scale synthetic dataset of 3 million points using the MuJoCo ( Todorov et al., 2012 ) physics simulation environment.

因此，我们利用 MuJoCo 物理仿真环境（Todorov 等，2012）生成了包含 300 万个回合的新合成数据集。

We develop an iterative “stitching” algorithm.

我们开发了一种迭代拼接算法。

We first simulate a pre-serve ball toss; at its apex, we query a large data pool of initial conditions of serves from ( D’Ambrosio et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) for the closest match in position.

首先模拟发球前的抛球；在抛球最高点，从已有发球初始条件的大型数据池中查询位置最接近的样本（D’Ambrosio 等，2025；Kienzle 等，2026）。

This serve is rolled out and from its terminal state we again query a large data pool of standard segments.

随后模拟该发球过程，并根据其终止状态再次查询标准击球片段的大型数据池。

By iteratively matching and stitching these sampled trajectory segments, we produce continuous, physically plausible sequences that enable training our network on full unsegmented points.

通过反复匹配并拼接采样得到的轨迹片段，我们生成连续且符合物理规律的序列，从而能够用完整、未经分割的回合训练网络。

Dense Spin Predictions.

逐帧旋转预测。

We adapt the baseline network architecture to exploit this new continuous data.

我们调整基线网络结构，以利用这些新的连续数据。

The architecture is illustrated in the supplementary material.

网络结构见补充材料。

The baseline model uses a learnable “spin token” to aggregate information and predicts a single initial spin vector ω→​(t0) for the input segment.

基线使用一个可学习的“旋转词元”聚合信息，为输入片段预测单个初始旋转向量 ω→​(t0)。

This is no longer feasible for processing full points, as the segments in the point are not known.

处理完整回合时，各击球片段尚未确定，因此这一设计不再适用。

We therefore remove this specialized token entirely.

所以，我们完全移除了这一专用词元。

Instead, we modify the network to predict spin in a dense, per-frame manner by applying a small MLP head to every output token of the transformer.

取而代之的是：在 Transformer 的每个输出词元上接入一个小型 MLP 预测头，以密集、逐帧的方式预测旋转。

To encourage the network to learn robust trajectory and spin dynamics for points of arbitrary length, we sample a subsequence (with a random length between 20 and 250 frames) from each full point in our synthetic dataset.

为让网络学习任意长度回合中鲁棒的轨迹和旋转动力学，我们从合成数据集的每个完整回合中采样一个子序列，其长度在 20 至 250 帧之间随机选取。

Interpolation Token.

插值词元。

Ball detections are frequently missing due to occlusions in oblique views of gameplay.

在斜视比赛画面中，遮挡经常导致球检测缺失。

While the baseline architecture ( Kienzle et al., 2026 ) simply ignores these detections, we treat the recovery of missing frames as a Masked Token Modeling (MTM) task.

基线结构（Kienzle 等，2026）直接忽略这类检测，而我们将缺失帧的恢复视为掩码词元建模（MTM）任务。

To prevent the loss of spatial camera context when a ball is missing, we introduce a Disentangled Context Embedding (DCE) .

为防止球缺失时丢失相机的空间上下文，我们引入解耦上下文嵌入（DCE）。

The 2D ball position and the table keypoints are projected into a higher dimension vector via separate linear layers.

二维球位置与球台关键点分别经过独立的线性层，投影到更高维的向量空间。

For frames with missing ball detections, we replace only the ball vector with a learnable interpolation token, leaving the projected table keypoints intact to preserve the camera information.

对缺少球检测的帧，仅用可学习的插值词元替换球位置向量，同时保留投影后的球台关键点，以维持相机信息。

Finally, we concatenate the ball vector and table keypoint vector and apply a linear layer to obtain the final embedding for each frame.

最后，拼接球位置向量与球台关键点向量，再经过一个线性层，得到每帧的最终嵌入。

This is illustrated in the supplementary material.

补充材料给出了示意图。

To prevent polluting the valid information of successful ball detections with the interpolation tokens, we integrate Deferred Upsampling Token Attention (DUTA) ( Einfalt et al., 2023 ) .

为避免插值词元干扰有效球检测的信息，我们引入延迟上采样词元注意力 DUTA（Einfalt 等，2023）。

DUTA applies masking in the initial transformer layer to prevent context dilution.

DUTA 在初始 Transformer 层施加注意力掩码，防止上下文信息被稀释。

Each token is only allowed to attend to tokens representing valid detections, ensuring that the tokens representing invalid detections can gather the necessary context without diluting the information of the valid tokens.

每个词元只允许关注有效检测对应的词元；这样，无效检测词元能够获取必要上下文，同时不会稀释有效词元的信息。

During training, we randomly mask valid 2D detections using empirically-derived occlusion probabilities (discussed in supplementary materials) and compute the dense 3D reconstruction loss over the entire trajectory, including the masked frames.

训练时，我们按经验得到的遮挡概率随机遮蔽有效二维检测，并对整条轨迹（包括遮蔽帧）计算密集三维重建损失；遮挡概率的细节见补充材料。

This forces the network to internalize the underlying physical constraints of ball motion to accurately in-paint missing segments.

这促使网络学习球运动背后的物理约束，以准确补全缺失片段。

3.5. Stage 3: 3D-Domain Annotation3.5.第 3 阶段：3D 域标注

With an unambiguous 3D ball trajectory now available, we can perform time segmentation and annotation directly in the 3D domain.

现在有了明确的 3D 球轨迹，我们可以直接在 3D 域中执行时间分段和标注。

Robust 3D-based Time Segmentation.

强大的基于 3D 的时间分段。

Our Lift-First Pipeline transforms time segmentation from a complex, 2D image-level tracking problem into an unambiguous, 1D signal analysis task in world coordinate space.

我们的 Lift-First Pipeline 将时间分割从复杂的 2D 图像级跟踪问题转换为世界坐标空间中明确的 1D 信号分析任务。

We identify hit events as the peaks and troughs in the ball’s 3D x -coordinates, using simple time and distance heuristics to filter local noise.

我们将击球事件识别为球的 3D x 坐标中的波峰和波谷，使用简单的时间和距离启发法来过滤局部噪声。

Similarly, we label table bounces as the local minima in the z-coordinates.

类似地，我们将表格反弹标记为 z 坐标中的局部最小值。

This provides the time segmentations that prior methods failed to reliably achieve.

这提供了先前方法无法可靠实现的时间分段。

Racket Stroke Estimation.

球拍行程估计。

Estimating a player’s 3D body pose provides useful cues for anticipating ball motion, but current human-pose estimators do not reliably capture hand orientation or wrist articulation.

估计球员的 3D 身体姿态为预测球运动提供了有用的线索，但当前的人体姿态估计器无法可靠地捕获手部方向或手腕关节。

This limitation is critical: the racket orientation at impact strongly determines the outgoing ball trajectory and spin.

这一限制至关重要：击球时的球拍方向很大程度上决定了球的出球轨迹和旋转。

Prior attempts to estimate racket pose directly from video ( Gao et al., 2019 ; Wang and Shi, 2013 ) remain insufficiently accurate or robust outside controlled laboratory settings.

之前直接从视频估计球拍姿态的尝试（Gao et al., 2019；Wang and Shi, 2013）在外部受控实验室设置下仍然不够准确或稳健。

Instead, we infer the racket state indirectly from the 3D ball trajectory.

相反，我们从 3D 球轨迹间接推断球拍状态。

When the ball’s flight time is short and its spin remains within a moderate range, the two-point boundary-value problem defined by the hit position and the subsequent table bounce admits a unique physically plausible ball trajectory ( Liu et al., 2012 ) .

当球的飞行时间很短并且其旋转保持在中等范围内时，由击球位置和随后的球台弹跳定义的两点边值问题承认独特的物理上合理的球轨迹（Liu et al., 2012）。

This provides the required ball velocity and spin immediately after impact.

这可以在击球后立即提供所需的球速和旋转。

Implementation and validation details are provided in the supplementary material.

补充材料中提供了实施和验证细节。

We use this procedure to augment our dataset with physically consistent racket-stroke parameters.

我们使用此过程通过物理上一致的球拍行程参数来扩充我们的数据集。

3.6. Stage 4: Filtering and Curation3.6.第四阶段：筛选与整理

The final stage enforces visual and physical consistency across all reconstructed rallies in order to keep only high quality reconstruction for our TT4D dataset.

最后阶段强制所有重建循环的视觉和物理一致性，以便为我们的 TT4D 数据集保留高质量的重建。

We apply one 2D-based filter and two 3D-based filters. 2D Reprojection Check.

我们应用一个基于 2D 的滤波器和两个基于 3D 的滤波器。 2D重投影检查。

We compare the reprojected 3D trajectory estimate to the original detections and normalize errors by the pixel length of the table diagonal.

我们将重投影的 3D 轨迹估计与原始检测进行比较，并通过表对角线的像素长度对误差进行归一化。

If the maximum normalized error exceeds a strict threshold (20% of the table diagonal length), the point is rejected.

如果最大归一化误差超过严格阈值（表对角线长度的 20%），则该点将被拒绝。

Figure 3 (b) shows the distribution of this error.

图 3 (b) 显示了该误差的分布。

Event Plausibility: We enforce game logic by analyzing the 3D path, ensuring it contains a valid sequence of events: clearly identifiable hit points and a single table bounce per segment (or two for serves).

事件合理性：我们通过分析 3D 路径来强化游戏逻辑，确保它包含有效的事件序列：清晰可辨的击球点和每段的单个球台弹跳（或发球两次）。

Physical Consistency: We assess physical consistency by fitting the ODE ( ODE ) constrained ball trajectory ( Gossard et al., 2025 ) to our network’s 3D output.

物理一致性：我们通过将 ODE (ODE) 约束球轨迹 (Gossard et al., 2025) 拟合到我们网络的 3D 输出来评估物理一致性。

This step effectively projects our prediction onto the manifold of physically possible trajectories.

这一步有效地将我们的预测投射到多种物理上可能的轨迹上。

If the maximum Euclidean distance between the prediction and the ODE fit exceeds a 30 cm threshold, the point is discarded as physically implausible.

如果预测和 ODE 拟合之间的最大欧几里得距离超过 30 cm 阈值，则该点将被丢弃，因为物理上不可信。

误差分布如图3(a)所示。

4.1. General Information4.1.一般信息

The TT4D dataset is sourced from 45,946 broadcast table tennis games (best of five, first to three) from 2021-2024.

TT4D 数据集源自 2021 年至 2024 年 45,946 场转播的乒乓球比赛（五局三胜、第一到三场）。

我们支持一般的固定相机姿态、单打和双打游戏，以及至少 25 FPS 的视频速度，最终产生 211,534 个重建点和 146 小时的游戏时间。

为了进行比较，LATTE-MV 数据集基于一组 1017 个游戏，需要特定的相机姿态，仅处理单人游戏，最终产生 23,782 个重建点和 26 小时的游戏时间。

Our scalability stems directly from the Lift-First Pipeline.

我们的可扩展性直接源于 Lift-First Pipeline。

By lifting the full unsegmented point directly to 3D, hit points and bounces can be identified robustly in world coordinates, even under heavy occlusion.

通过三维提升，将完整的未分段点直接转为 3D，即使在严重遮挡的情况下，也可以在世界坐标中可靠地识别击球点和弹跳。

Moreover, because the 3D trajectory is reconstructed independently of human-pose tracking, the method naturally generalizes to doubles matches.

此外，由于 3D 轨迹的重建独立于人体姿态跟踪，因此该方法自然可以推广到双打匹配。

A further advantage from a game-theoretic standpoint is that the final winning shot of each point — unrecoverable in 2D-based time segmentation pipelines — is reliably reconstructed.

从博弈论的角度来看，另一个优点是每个点的最终获胜击球（在基于 2D 的时间分段流程中无法恢复）可以可靠地重建。

We also require a minimum of two segments to be reconstructed for each point, and we require an average ball visibility of at least 50%.

6.1. Generative Model of Competitive Gameplay6.1.竞技比赛的生成模型

As a key downstream application, we utilize the fidelity and diversity of our dataset for training a generative model of competitive gameplay.

作为关键的下游应用程序，我们利用数据集的保真度和多样性来训练竞技比赛的生成模型。

High-quality, diverse generated samples serve as strong evidence that our TT4D dataset captures the underlying structure of the sport.

高质量、多样化的生成样本有力地证明我们的 TT4D 数据集捕获了这项运动的底层结构。

Generative Framework.

生成框架。

We adopt the Conditional Flow Matching (CFM) framework ( Lipman et al., 2023 ; Tong et al., 2024a ; Tong et al., 2024b ) .

我们采用条件流匹配（CFM）框架（Lipman et al., 2023; Tong et al., 2024a; Tong et al., 2024b）。

Let 𝒟 be our dataset of reconstructed trajectories, p0=𝒩⁡(0,I) be a base distribution, and pdata be the empirical distribution over 𝒟 .

令𝒟为我们的重建轨迹数据集，p0=𝒩⁡(0,I)为基础分布，pdata为𝒟上的经验分布。

Let τ denote a horizon of 20 observations, and c denote a history of 10 observations.

让τ表示20个观测值的范围，c表示10个观测值的历史。

We learn a conditional vector field vθ whose ODE solution d​τtd​t=vθ​(τt,t∣c) transports p0 onto pdata .

我们学习一个条件向量场vθ，其ODE解d​τtd​t=vθ​(τt,t∣c)将p0传输到pdata。

We use the flow-matching loss ℒCFM=𝔼⁡[‖vθ​(τt,t∣c)−ut‖22] , where τt is a simple interpolation between samples from p0 and pdata , and ut is the target velocity.

我们使用流匹配损失ℒCFM=𝔼⁡[‖vθ​(τt,t∣c)−ut‖22]，其中τt是p0和pdata样本之间的简单插值，ut是目标速度。

This conditional formulation enables the model to generate full 20-step future trajectories with high physical fidelity given past context.

这种条件公式使模型能够在给定过去背景的情况下生成具有高物理保真度的完整 20 步未来轨迹。

Full details on the model architecture and training hyperparameters are in the supplementary material.

有关模型架构和训练超参数的完整详细信息位于补充材料中。

Evaluation.

评估。

We generate 10,000 multi-segment rallies by autoregressively rolling out the model: starting from a 10-observation history from our dataset, we generate the next 20 observations and then repeatedly invoke the flow model on the 10 most recent generated observations.

我们通过自回归推出模型来生成 10,000 个多分段循环：从数据集中的 10 个观察历史记录开始，我们生成接下来的 20 个观察结果，然后对最近生成的 10 个观察结果重复调用流模型。

These rallies are clipped using the same 3D-based time segmentation scheme outlined in Section 3.5 .

这些圆圈使用第 3.5 节中概述的相同的基于 3D 的时间分段方案进行剪辑。

Only 6 out of the 10,000 generated rallies failed the time segmentation stage.

在生成的 10,000 个循环中，只有 6 个未能通过时间分段阶段。

We evaluate the generated rallies using the same rigorous filtering and diversity metrics which we used to create the dataset.

我们使用与创建数据集相同的严格过滤和多样性指标来评估生成的循环。

As shown in Figure 8 , the distribution of the Physics-Based ODE Fit error for our generated samples (Gen Mean: 8.72 cm) is remarkably similar to and even slightly better than that of the real data (Data Mean: 10.77 cm).

如图 8 所示，我们生成的样本的基于物理的 ODE 拟合误差的分布（Gen 平均值：8.72 cm）与真实数据的分布（数据平均值：10.77 cm）非常相似，甚至略好一些。

This confirms that our generative model, trained on TT4D, produces physically plausible trajectories.

这证实了我们在 TT4D 上训练的生成模型可以产生物理上合理的轨迹。

We also demonstrate proper alignment in inter-hit time distribution.

我们还展示了命中间时间分布的正确对齐。

Though the generated gameplay distribution exhibits less spread, it nonetheless exhibits speeds at both ends of the real-world spectrum.

尽管生成的游戏玩法分布范围较小，但它仍然在现实世界范围的两端表现出速度。

Hence, we should expect a broad class of behaviors in our dataset.

因此，我们应该期待数据集中出现广泛的行为。

6.2. Racket Reconstruction6.2.球拍重建

Our high-fidelity 3D ball trajectories and dense spin vectors provide a robust foundation for reconstructing racket motion.

我们的高保真 3D 球轨迹和密集旋转矢量为重建球拍运动提供了坚实的基础。

Since direct monocular tracking of the racket is often unreliable due to its high velocity, small size, and frequent occlusions, we leverage the reconstructed ball state instead.

由于球拍的高速度、小尺寸和频繁遮挡，直接单目跟踪通常不可靠，因此我们利用重建的球状态来代替。

We obtain the racket stroke parameters, defined as the racket’s velocity and orientation at impact, from solving an inverse control problem.

我们通过求解逆控制问题获得球拍行程参数，定义为球拍撞击时的速度和方向。

By optimizing these stroke parameters such that the simulated post-impact ball trajectory accurately matches our observed 3D trajectory and bounce timing, we can recover the racket stroke that produced the observed ball trajectory.

通过优化这些击球参数，使模拟的击球后球轨迹与我们观察到的 3D 轨迹和弹跳时间精确匹配，我们可以恢复产生观察到的球轨迹的球拍击球。

We evaluate our approach against motion capture ground truth using infrared markers on the racket, across 92 recorded strokes.

我们使用球拍上的红外标记在 92 个记录的击球中评估我们针对运动捕捉真值的方法。

We obtain a mean orientation error of 26.4±4.4∘ and a velocity error of 0.58±0.40 m/s in the world frame, for an average impact speed of 3.72 m/s.

我们在世界坐标系中获得了26.4±4.4∘的平均方向误差和0.58±0.40m/s的速度误差，平均撞击速度为3.72 m/s。

The residual error is mainly in the world-frame Z-velocity and rotation about the X-axis (racket open/closed angle), likely due to unknown racket bounce properties.

残余误差主要存在于世界坐标系 Z 轴速度和绕 X 轴的旋转（球拍打开/关闭角度），可能是由于未知的球拍弹跳特性。

Furthermore, TT4D provides the necessary data for training behavior-aware models, such as anticipatory robotic policies, allowing agents to reason about the strategic decisions made by other players.

此外，TT4D 还提供了训练行为感知模型所需的数据，例如预期的机器人策略，允许代理推理其他参与者做出的战略决策。

7. Conclusion7. 结论

We presented the Lift-First Pipeline for multimodal sports reconstruction from monocular videos, which lifts entire unsegmented rallies to 3D before performing any time segmentation.

我们提出了用于从单目视频进行多模式运动重建的提升优先流程，该流程在执行任何时间分割之前将整个未分段的循环提升到 3D。

By working in the 3D domain, our approach avoids the fragility of 2D-based time segmentation under occlusions and missing detections.

通过在 3D 域中工作，我们的方法避免了基于 2D 的时间分割在遮挡和缺失检测下的脆弱性。

This is enabled by a Full-Sequence Lifting Network trained on a large-scale synthetic dataset of 3 M full points that also models the pre-serve toss.

这是通过全序列三维提升网络实现的，该网络在3M个完整回合的大规模合成数据集上进行训练，该数据集也对发球前投掷进行了建模。

While demonstrated on table tennis, our general pipeline can be readily extended to other sports such as tennis or badminton.

虽然在乒乓球上进行了演示，但我们的通用流程可以轻松扩展到其他运动，例如网球或羽毛球。

The result of this pipeline is the TT4D dataset , a 140+ hour high-fidelity multimodal dataset with 3D ball trajectories, 3D human meshes, and, for the first time at this scale, 3D spin vectors and precise 3D-derived time segmentations.

该流程的结果是 TT4D 数据集，这是一个 140 多个小时的高保真多模式数据集，具有 3D 球轨迹、3D 人体网格，并且首次在这种规模上提供 3D 自旋向量和精确的 3D 派生时间分段。

We demonstrated the fidelity and utility of TT4D through physics-based validation and downstream applications, including racket-contact estimation and a generative model of competitive rallies.

我们通过基于物理的验证和下游应用（包括球拍接触估计和竞争性循环生成模型）展示了 TT4D 的保真度和实用性。

A.1. Video Clipping and DeduplicationA.1.视频剪辑和重复数据删除

Video Clipping Our reconstruction pipeline starts with a set of multi-hour, uncut table tennis competition videos.

视频剪辑 我们的重建流程从一组长达数小时、未剪辑的乒乓球比赛视频开始。

This is processed into a set of reconstructed points by first progressing through two stages of clipping.

首先通过两个剪辑阶段将其处理为一组重建点。

The first stage clips the broadcast video at frames when the scoreboard advances.

第一阶段在记分牌前进时按帧剪辑转播视频。

We detect the change in score by first identifying the scoreboard using YOLO ( Varghese and M., 2024 ) and then recovering its text using PaddleOCR ( Cui et al., 2025 ) .

我们首先使用 YOLO 识别记分板（Varghese 和 M.，2024），然后使用 PaddleOCR 恢复其文本（Cui 等人，2025）来检测分数的变化。

The text associated with the player names also allows us to discern singles from doubles gameplay.

与球员姓名相关的文本也使我们能够区分单打和双打比赛。

This scoreboard-based clip contains a significant number of frames preceding and following the actual gameplay.

这个基于记分板的剪辑包含实际游戏之前和之后的大量帧。

To trim the clips down further, the second stage identifies the approximate start and end of the point by using the 2D ball trajectory, b2D​(n) .

为了进一步修剪剪辑，第二阶段使用 2D 球轨迹b2D​(n)来识别该点的大致起点和终点。

For this stage, we use the LATTE-MV ball tracker ( Etaat et al., 2025 ) to obtain b2D​(n) .

对于这个阶段，我们使用 LATTE-MV 球跟踪器 (Etaat et al., 2025) 来获得b2D​(n)。

This ball tracker uses the full TracknetV3 model, which includes the inpainting module.

该球跟踪器使用完整的 TracknetV3 模型，其中包括修复模块。

Note that the inpainting module is removed when we compute b2D​(n) for reconstruction.

请注意，当我们计算b2D​(n)进行重建时，修复模块被删除。

The key idea of this stage is that while the oscillations of b2D​(n) cannot reliably solve hit-point identification, it can be used to determine the approximate start and end of the point.

这一阶段的关键思想是，虽然b2D​(n)的振荡不能可靠地解决击球点识别问题，但它可以用来确定该点的大致起点和终点。

This procedure is outlined in Algorithm 1 .

算法 1 概述了该过程。

Note that this stage may fail and produce clips that do not exhibit any gameplay.

请注意，此阶段可能会失败并生成不展示任何游戏玩法的剪辑。

This is not a problem, however, since these clips are removed in the filtering stage.

然而，这不是问题，因为这些剪辑在过滤阶段被移除。

Duplicated Frame Removal While processing online table-tennis footage, we observed that certain frames were duplicated within the video stream.

重复帧删除在处理在线乒乓球镜头时，我们观察到某些帧在视频流中重复。

This phenomenon typically arises when the frame rate used for camera capture differs from that used during rendering or re-encoding.

当用于相机捕获的帧速率与渲染或重新编码期间使用的帧速率不同时，通常会出现这种现象。

In such cases, the renderer/encoder fills missing timestamps by repeating the temporally nearest frames, thereby matching the target output frame rate.

在这种情况下，渲染器/编码器通过重复时间上最近的帧来填充丢失的时间戳，从而匹配目标输出帧速率。

We identified three cases: (i) periodic duplicated frames, characterized by a first duplicated index s and a duplication period f , (ii) aperiodic duplicated frames, and (iii) no duplicated frames.

我们确定了三种情况：(i) 周期性重复帧，其特征是第一个重复索引s和重复周期f，(ii) 非周期性重复帧，以及 (iii) 无重复帧。

The periodic case was the most common in the processed videos.

在处理的视频中，周期性情况是最常见的。

Although imperceptible to the human eye, this frame-duplication artifact causes the tracked ball to appear at the same position for two consecutive frames.

虽然人眼无法察觉，但这种帧重复伪影会导致跟踪球在连续两个帧中出现在同一位置。

As a consequence, the reconstruction algorithm falsely interprets these repetitions as sudden deceleration or temporary stops, which deteriorates trajectory estimation accuracy.

因此，重建算法错误地将这些重复解释为突然减速或暂时停止，这降低了轨迹估计的准确性。

A direct pixel-wise absolute difference is not a reliable measure of frame similarity.

直接的像素级绝对差并不是帧相似性的可靠度量。

Even when two frames are visually identical, their difference image resembles approximately white noise due to encoder and compression artifacts.

即使两帧视觉上相同，由于编码器和压缩伪影，它们的差异图像也近似白噪声。

The aggregated absolute difference over all pixels in such cases can be comparable to that between genuinely different consecutive frames.

在这种情况下，所有像素的聚合绝对差可以与真正不同的连续帧之间的绝对差进行比较。

This motivates the use of SSIM , which evaluates perceptual similarity by comparing luminance, contrast, and structural content.

A.2. Camera CalibrationA.2.相机标定

The camera parameters are not provided for online videos, so the calibration must be performed without a dedicated calibration pattern.

在线视频不提供相机参数，因此必须在没有专用校准图案的情况下进行校准。

We therefore adopt the TT3D approach ( Gossard et al., 2025 ) , which uses the table itself as the calibration object.

因此，我们采用 TT3D 方法（Gossard et al., 2025），该方法使用桌子本身作为校准对象。

The world frame is defined by the standard table geometry, and the four table corners serve as known 3D reference points.

世界坐标系由标准球台几何形状定义，四个球台角用作已知的 3D 参考点。

A segmentation model first extracts the table mask, after which table edges are detected using a Hough line transform and intersected to obtain the corner locations.

分割模型首先提取表格掩码，然后使用霍夫线变换检测表格边缘并相交以获得角位置。

With these correspondences, the camera extrinsics, rotation 𝐑 and translation 𝐓 relative to the table frame, are estimated jointly with the focal length f .

通过这些对应关系，相机外参、相对于工作台框架的旋转𝐑和平移𝐓与焦距f一起被估计。

The problem is formulated as a Perspective- n -Point task with unknown focal length and solved by iteratively minimizing the reprojection error over (𝐑,𝐓,f) , enabling robust calibration even under partial occlusions and in unconstrained broadcast footage.

该问题被表述为焦距未知的透视-n-点任务，并通过迭代最小化(𝐑,𝐓,f)上的重投影误差来解决，即使在部分遮挡和不受约束的广播镜头中也能实现稳健的校准。

In contrast to the original algorithm in ( Gossard et al., 2025 ) , our segmentation model is based on the UNet++ architecture ( Zhou et al., 2018 ) with the bigger Efficientnet-B0 backbone ( Tan and Le, 2019 ) .

与（Gossard 等人，2025）中的原始算法相比，我们的分割模型基于 UNet++ 架构（Zhou 等人，2018），具有更大的 Efficientnet-B0 主干网（Tan 和 Le，2019）。

For training, we use a combination of Binary Cross Entropy and DICE Loss.

对于训练，我们结合使用二元交叉熵和 DICE 损失。

With these modifications, we are able to obtain high quality camera calibrations for our dataset.

通过这些修改，我们能够为我们的数据集获得高质量的相机标定。

To effectively integrate these interpolation tokens into the transformer without degrading the feature quality of observed frames, we adopt the Deferred Upsampling Token Attention (DUTA) mechanism ( Einfalt et al., 2023 ) .

为了有效地将这些插值词元集成到Transformer中，而不降低观察到的帧的特征质量，我们采用了延迟上采样词元注意（DUTA）机制（Einfalt et al., 2023）。

Since the learnable interpolation tokens initially contain no specific ball information beyond their temporal embedding, allowing valid location tokens to attend to them in the first layers can introduce significant noise and corrupt the high-fidelity representations of detected ball positions.

由于可学习插值标记最初不包含超出其时间嵌入的特定球信息，因此允许有效位置标记在第一层中处理它们可能会引入显着的噪声并破坏检测到的球位置的高保真表示。

B.2. Synthetic Dataset GenerationB.2.合成数据集生成

A network designed to process full points requires training data that reflects this new, more complex structure.

设计用于处理完整回合的网络需要反映这种新的、更复杂的结构的训练数据。

In contrast, previous methods ( Kienzle et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) are only trained on smaller datasets of only 50k-140k individual segments, which can only be obtained from unreliable 2D-based time segmentation during inference.

相比之下，以前的方法（Kienzle et al., 2025；Kienzle et al., 2026）仅在只有 50k-140k 个单独片段的较小数据集上进行训练，这些数据集只能在推理过程中从不可靠的基于 2D 的时间分割中获得。

To power our lifting network, we generate a new massive-scale synthetic dataset of approximately 3 million points , with each point consisting of multiple stitched segments.

为了为我们的三维提升网络提供动力，我们生成了一个包含约 300 万个回合的新的大规模合成数据集，每个点由多个缝合段组成。

This dataset utilizes the MuJoCo ( Todorov et al., 2012 ) physics simulation environment, ensuring all trajectories are physically correct and include perfect, per-frame ground truth for 3D position, and spin.

该数据集利用 MuJoCo（Todorov 等人，2012）物理模拟环境，确保所有轨迹在物理上都是正确的，并包括 3D 位置和旋转的完美每帧真值。

Simulating 3 million complete end-to-end points from purely random initial conditions is computationally infeasible.

从纯随机初始条件模拟 300 万个完整的端到端点在计算上是不可行的。

To overcome this, we present a "stitching" pipeline, detailed in Algorithm 2 , that assembles points from a large pool of pre-validated, physically-correct segments.

为了克服这个问题，我们提出了一个“缝合”流程，详见算法 2，它从大量预先验证的、物理上正确的段中组装点。

The authors of ( D’Ambrosio et al., 2025 ) and ( Kienzle et al., 2026 ) provide a large collection of initial conditions for serves and standard shots, which we utilize as a starting point for our pool.

(D’Ambrosio et al., 2025) 和 (Kienzle et al., 2026) 的作者提供了大量发球和标准击球的初始条件，我们将其作为我们池的起点。

Simulating a full point then becomes a recursive, hybrid process: First, we simulate an initial segment (e.g., a serve) and find its final 3D position r→N−1 3​D .

然后，模拟一个完整的点就变成了一个递归的混合过程：首先，我们模拟一个初始片段（例如，发球）并找到其最终的 3D 位置r→N−1 3​D。

This becomes the initial position for the next segment.

这将成为下一段的初始位置。

To determine the segments initial spin and velocity, we find the segment in our data pool whose initial position is the closest match to our current state.

为了确定段的初始旋转和速度，我们在数据池中找到初始位置与当前状态最接近的段。

We then simulate the trajectory and check if it is a valid table tennis shot segment (i.e., it clears the net and bounces on the opponent’s side).

然后我们模拟轨迹并检​​查它是否是有效的乒乓球击球片段（即，它过网并在对手一侧弹跳）。

If valid, we "stitch" this new segment to our point and repeat the process until the point is complete.

如果有效，我们将这个新段“缝合”到我们的点上，并重复该过程直到该点完成。

Furthermore, we identify that a major failure point for generalization to real-world data is the pre-serve ball toss , a phase that is difficult to clip before lifting.

此外，我们发现推广到现实世界数据的一个主要失败点是发球前抛球，这是一个在三维提升之前很难剪辑的阶段。

To make our network robust to this, we explicitly simulate an entirely new pool of synthetic "throw" segments.

为了使我们的网络对此具有鲁棒性，我们明确模拟了一个全新的合成“投掷”段池。

Our final training points are assembled by first simulating a "throw" segment, stitching a "serve" segment from our pool to its apex, and then recursively stitching the subsequent return segments.

Unlike optimization-based approaches ( Etaat et al., 2025 ; Gossard et al., 2025 ; Liu and Wang, 2022 ) , which require a computationally expensive fitting process for every individual segment of a point, learning-based methods ( Kienzle et al., 2025 ; Kienzle et al., 2026 ) perform lifting via a single, efficient forward pass.

While the initial training cost is non-negligible (approximately 2 days on a single NVIDIA H100 GPU), the resulting model offers exceptional efficiency during inference.

虽然初始训练成本不可忽略（在单个 NVIDIA H100 GPU 上大约需要 2 天），但生成的模型在推理过程中提供了卓越的效率。

Our proposed network further enhances this efficiency by processing entire points consisting of multiple segments in a single forward pass, rather than lifting each segment individually.

我们提出的网络通过在一次前向传递中处理由多个段组成的整个点，而不是单独处理每个段的三维窗口，进一步提高了这种效率。

We evaluate the inference speed of our Lifting Network across three generations of GPU hardware: NVIDIA H100, V100, and Titan X (Pascal).

我们评估了三维提升网络络在三代 GPU 硬件上的推理速度：NVIDIA H100、V100 和 Titan X (Pascal)。

We distinguish between two operating regimes: an online mode (batch size 1), representing latency-critical real-time applications, and an offline mode (batch size 128), representing high-throughput dataset generation.

我们区分两种操作模式：在线模式（批量大小 1），代表延迟关键型实时应用程序；离线模式（批量大小 128），代表高吞吐量数据集生成。

在 Titan X (Pascal) 上，网络在在线模式下每秒处理 25 个点。

Given that a typical table tennis point lasts several seconds, this inference speed is orders of magnitude faster than real-time, enabling low-latency applications such as live broadcasting analysis or robotic anticipation on consumer-grade hardware.

鉴于典型的乒乓球点持续几秒钟，这种推理速度比实时速度快几个数量级，从而支持低延迟应用，例如消费级硬件上的直播分析或机器人预测。

K.5. Humanoid Motion Tracking of Table Tennis MotionsK.5。乒乓球运动的人形机器人动作跟踪

We validate the fidelity of our Player Reconstruction algorithm ( A.3 ) by replaying the motion on a Unitree G1 robot.

我们通过在 Unitree G1 机器人上重放运动来验证球员重建算法 (A.3) 的保真度。

First, we retarget the SMPL motion to the Unitree G1 using GMR ( Araujo et al., 2026 ) .

首先，我们使用 GMR 将 SMPL 运动重新定位到 Unitree G1（Araujo 等人，2026）。

Next, to facilitate smooth hardware deployment, we use the motion in-betweening capabilities of GEM ( Li et al., 2025 ) to ease the start and end of each motion.

接下来，为了促进硬件部署的顺利进行，我们使用 GEM 的运动中间功能（Li et al., 2025）来简化每个运动的开始和结束。

Specifically, we generate half a second of motion to smoothly transition from an A-pose to the initial motion pose and from the final motion pose back to an A-pose.

具体来说，我们生成半秒的运动，以平滑地从 A 姿态过渡到初始运动姿态，并从最终运动姿态平滑地过渡回 A 姿态。

Finally, we train a motion tracking policy on this smoothed motion using BeyondMimic ( Liao et al., 2025 ) for 30,000 iterations on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPU.

最后，我们使用 BeyondMimic（Liao 等人，2025）在 NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 GPU 上训练这种平滑运动的运动跟踪策略，进行 30,000 次迭代。
